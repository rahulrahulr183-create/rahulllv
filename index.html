ABOUT THE GRAMA PANCHAYAT

A Village of Heritage and Community Development

Bharanikkavu Grama Panchayat is a local self-government institution in Mavelikkara Taluk of Alappuzha District, Kerala. The Kayamkulam–Punalur K.P. Road passes through the panchayat area.

The region has a rich historical and cultural background. Its name is associated with the ancient Bharanikkavu Devi Temple. An ancient Buddha statue discovered in the area is preserved in the temple premises, according to the panchayat profile.

The panchayat includes settlements such as Kattanam and Bharanikkavu and has a history connected with agriculture, education and community life.

PHYSICAL AND SPATIAL PROFILE

Geographical Characteristics

A brief profile of the location, landscape, climate context and connectivity of Bharanikkavu Grama Panchayat.

📍

Geographical Location

Bharanikkavu is situated in the southern part of Alappuzha District, in Mavelikkara Taluk. It is part of Bharanikkavu Block Panchayat.

🗺️

Administrative Profile

The panchayat comes under Alappuzha District and Bharanikkavu Block. The official panchayat history page lists Kattanam and Bharanikkavu villages.

🌱

Agricultural Background

The panchayat history describes an earlier agrarian landscape with paddy fields and coconut groves. Agriculture was an important part of the local economy.

🌦️

Climate

Bharanikkavu is in Kerala, which has a tropical monsoon climate. For ward-level rainfall and temperature values, local meteorological data should be added.

🛣️

Transport and Connectivity

The Kayamkulam–Punalur K.P. Road passes through the panchayat area and connects settlements in the region.

🏛️

Historical and Cultural Places

The official panchayat portal highlights Bharanikkavu Devi Temple, Vettikode Nagaraja Temple, Kattanam Valiyaveedu Devi Temple and Kattanam St. Stephen’s Orthodox Church.

Administrative and Geographical Boundaries

  • East: Thamarakulam and Chunakkara Grama Panchayats
  • West: Krishnapuram and Chettikulangara Grama Panchayats
  • North: Thekkekara Grama Panchayat
  • South: Vallikunnam Grama Panchayat

Boundary information is based on the official Bharanikkavu Grama Panchayat history page.

HISTORY AND CULTURE

The Cultural Identity of Bharanikkavu

Bharanikkavu has a distinctive cultural history in Central Travancore. The name is associated with the Bharanikkavu Devi Temple and its Meenabharani festival. The panchayat is also known for traditional festivals and cultural practices.

The official panchayat profile also identifies Vettikode Nagaraja Temple, Kattanam Valiyaveedu Devi Temple and Kattanam Valiyapalli as important local landmarks.
